Privacy Policy

fakinPillow Publisher · Effective October 17, 2026

fakinPillow Publisher (“the tool”) is a personal, single-user automation tool operated by the owner of the @fakinpillow YouTube channel and the @fakinpillow Instagram account. This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

Data the tool accesses

  • YouTube API Services. The tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the operator's own channel and to read metadata of its own uploads (for verification and reporting). Use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • OAuth credentials. The tool stores OAuth access and refresh tokens for the operator's own Google account, locally on the operator's device, solely to perform uploads on the operator's behalf.

Data the tool does not collect

  • No data is collected from third parties, viewers, or visitors to this site.
  • No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising is used.

Storage and revocation

Credentials are stored only on the operator's device and are not transmitted to any third-party server. Access can be revoked at any time via the Google account permissions page, after which the tool can no longer access the account.
